# Pollen Chakma — Academic & Research Website (`pollen-chakma.github.io`)

Personal academic portfolio and research group website for **Pollen Chakma**, Assistant Professor in the Department of Water Resources Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), Bangladesh.

## Structure

- [`index.html`](./index.html) — Home page (Hero, About, Research Interests, Featured Work, Recent News)
- [`research/index.html`](./research/index.html) — Research Program Diagram, Funded Projects ($18K), Applied Consulting & Drone Mapping Projects, Interactive Co-Author Network, and Research Lab / Supervised Students
- [`publications/index.html`](./publications/index.html) — Refereed Journal Articles, Preprints, and Conference Proceedings with instant search and category filters
- [`software/index.html`](./software/index.html) — Interactive Google Earth Engine (GEE) Cloud Web Applications & Computational Hydrology Toolkit
- [`teaching/index.html`](./teaching/index.html) — Undergraduate Theory & Laboratory Courses, World Bank EDGE Training Workshops, and Supervised Theses
- [`cv/index.html`](./cv/index.html) — Full Curriculum Vitae (Appointments, Education, Grants, Consulting, Awards, Skills, Test Scores, References)
- [`posts/index.html`](./posts/index.html) — Blog Posts, Field Stories, and Photo Gallery with Lightbox Preview
- [`contact/index.html`](./contact/index.html) — Contact Information, Academic Profiles, and Prospective Student Application Checklist

## Easy Content Updates

- **Update News:** Edit [`data/news.js`](./data/news.js)
- **Update Publications:** Edit [`data/publications.js`](./data/publications.js)
- **Styles & Theme:** Edit [`assets/css/style.css`](./assets/css/style.css)
