Undergraduate theory courses

Courses taught across the B.Sc. in Water Resources Engineering program (Outcome-Based Education curriculum).

WRE-303 · 2018 – Present

GIS and Remote Sensing in Water Resources Engineering

Principles of satellite remote sensing, spatial data models, DEM processing, watershed delineation, and geospatial applications in hydrology.

WRE-403 · 2018 – Present

Coastal Engineering

Wave mechanics, astronomical tides, tropical cyclone storm surges, coastal sediment transport, and shoreline protection design.

WRE-425 · 2019 – Present

Climatology

Atmospheric circulation, hydro-climatology, monsoon dynamics, climate variability, and extreme climate indices assessment.

WRE-451 · 2018

Integrated Water Resources Planning and Management

Systems analysis, multi-criteria decision-making (MCDM), sustainable basin planning, and water governance.

WRE-405 · 2019

River Engineering and Basin Management

Fluvial geomorphology, alluvial river mechanics, bank erosion-accretion dynamics, river training works, and basin management.

WRE-203 · 2016 – 2022

Fluid Mechanics

Fluid statics, kinematics, conservation of mass, momentum, and energy, boundary layers, and pipe/open-channel flow fundamentals.

WRE-201 · 2018 – 2019

Geology and Geomorphology

Physical geology, hydrogeological formations, landform evolution, and deltaic geomorphology of Bangladesh.

WRE-103 · 2017 – 2019

Surveying

Engineering surveying, leveling, total station traversing, topographic mapping, and modern GNSS/photogrammetric methods.

WRE-101 · 2018 – 2020

Analytical Mechanics

Statics of particles and rigid bodies, trusses, centroids, moments of inertia, friction, and engineering dynamics.
