SCIP Project: Drone Photogrammetry for Plastic Pollution Reduction

Conducted at CUET and funded by the German Government, this initiative generated high-resolution spatial evidence to inform waste management. As a Geospatial and Survey Consultant, I carried out detailed UAV drone surveys to identify, map, and assess 27 solid waste dumping sites using WebODM and QGIS.

May 23, 2019 · Field Projects

Kinitheu Water Supply System Design — Japan Government Funded Project

In the remote hill tracts of Bandarban, over 50 children at a residential child care center previously climbed down nearly 200–400 feet daily to fetch water from a mountain stream (jhiri). We designed a solar-powered water lifting pump, elevated gravity storage & sedimentation tanks, and rainwater harvesting system to bring safe drinking water directly to their premises.

Read Full Bandarban Field Story

With no municipal water connection and no grid electricity, solar energy became the most practical and sustainable choice to lift water from the jhiri to a high-elevation storage tank near the child care center. Pretreatment sedimentation and rooftop rainwater harvesting were integrated for dry-season water security—transforming daily health, safety, and study time for 50+ resident students.
