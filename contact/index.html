Opportunities

Undergraduate & graduate research opportunities

Are you an undergraduate or graduate student interested in gaining hands-on research experience? I welcome motivated students from Water Resources Engineering, Civil Engineering, Urban & Regional Planning, and related disciplines to join my research group and contribute to ongoing funded projects. Working with students is one of the most rewarding parts of my academic life—I believe research should be accessible, collaborative, and an opportunity to grow both academically and professionally.

Research areas where you can get involved

  • Storm Surge & Coastal Hazard Modelling (Delft3D, Delft3D-FM)
  • Hydrologic, Hydrodynamic & Urban Flood Modelling (HEC-RAS 2D, HEC-HMS, EPA-SWMM)
  • Satellite Flood Mapping & Climate Extremes (Sentinel-1 SAR, CHIRPS, ETCCDI indices)
  • Remote Sensing & Cloud Geospatial Analytics in Google Earth Engine (JavaScript & Python APIs)
  • AI / Machine Learning Applications in Water Resources Engineering

Who can apply & benefits of joining

  • No prior research experience is required — curiosity, persistence, and willingness to learn quantitative/modelling tools are essential.
  • Direct mentorship on model setup, Python/GEE coding, and scientific writing.
  • Co-authorship opportunities to present at international conferences and publish peer-reviewed journal articles.
  • Strong preparation for graduate school applications or specialized engineering careers.

Before you apply: Quick application checklist
